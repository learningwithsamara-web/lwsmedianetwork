📺 HOW TO JOIN TONIGHT
Follow the steps for your device below
📤 STEP 1 — SHARE THIS PAGE
1️⃣Download this file and send it via WhatsApp to your family group
2️⃣Tell them: "Open this file in Chrome or your browser — it's your cinema ticket!"
3️⃣Then follow the steps below to sync the movie on Netflix
💻
ON A LAPTOP OR COMPUTER
Chrome browser required
1
Open Google Chrome on your laptop (download it free if you don't have it)
2
Go to teleparty.com and click "Add to Chrome" — it's completely free
3
Open Netflix and sign in with your own account, then start the movie
4
Click the Teleparty icon (top right of Chrome) → click "Start the Party"
5
Copy the party link it gives you and send it to family on WhatsApp — they click it and everyone is in sync! 🎉
📱
ON A PHONE
No Teleparty needed — use WhatsApp
1
Teleparty does not work on phones — but don't worry, here's what to do
2
The host at home starts a WhatsApp Video Call with phone family members
3
On the video call, tap "Share Screen" → then open Netflix and play the movie
4
Phone family can see and hear the movie through the call — open this cinema page on the side for the full experience 🎬
💡 Pro tip: Start a WhatsApp group called "Samara's Cinema Night 🎬" before showtime. Share this file in the group, share the Teleparty link in the group, and use the group to do a countdown together. Family overseas can send birthday messages in the chat during the movie! 🎉
